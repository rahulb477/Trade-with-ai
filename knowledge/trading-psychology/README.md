## Why this belongs in a research repository

Psychology is usually taught as motivation. It should be taught as **error prevention**. Most trading losses come
from predictable, repeatable cognitive errors — not from a lack of indicators:

- Remembering the winners and forgetting the losers (which corrupts every self-assessment).
- Fitting rules to the last three trades (which is overfitting at the personal level).
- Taking a bigger position after a loss (which converts a survivable drawdown into a fatal one).
- Refusing to write down an invalidation (which makes the analysis unfalsifiable).

The countermeasures here are **procedural**, not motivational. That is the only kind that works.
