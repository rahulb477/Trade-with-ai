# Trend Phases

## 1. The four-phase model

A widely documented framing (associated with Wyckoff and with market-cycle literature):

```
1. ACCUMULATION  — sideways after a decline; supply is absorbed
2. MARKUP        — the uptrend; HH/HL; participation broadens
3. DISTRIBUTION  — sideways after an advance; demand is absorbed
4. MARKDOWN      — the downtrend; LH/LL
```

The phases repeat, but **not on a fixed clock**. Durations vary enormously. Any source that tells you a phase
lasts "6–8 weeks" is describing a particular market at a particular time.

## 2. How each phase actually looks

| Phase | Price | Volume | Structure | Oscillators |
|-------|-------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Accumulation | Range after decline; tests of support | Drying up, then spikes on down-tests that get absorbed | No HH/HL yet; equal lows common | RSI hovering 30–50 |
| Markup | Rising; HH/HL | Expanding on advances, contracting on pullbacks | Clean HH/HL | RSI holds 40–80 |
| Distribution | Range after advance; tests of resistance | Heavy on up-tests that fail; declining overall | No LH/LL yet; equal highs common | RSI hovering 50–70 |
| Markdown | Falling; LH/LL | Expanding on declines | Clean LH/LL | RSI holds 20–60 |

## 3. The honest limitation

**You cannot identify the phase in real time with certainty.** Accumulation and distribution are defined by what
happens *after* them. In the moment, a range is just a range.

What you *can* do:

1. **Describe** the range objectively (range width, duration, volume trend, position relative to prior trend).
2. **Assign a probability lean** ("this range follows a long advance on declining volume — consistent with
   distribution, but not confirmed").
3. **Define both resolutions** with triggers and invalidations.
4. **Refuse to state the phase as fact.**

Wording matters:
- ✅ "The range is consistent with distribution: it follows a 9-month advance, volume is declining, and the
  upper boundary has been rejected three times."
- ❌ "This is distribution."

## 4. Early vs mature trend — the most useful distinction

| Marker | Early trend | Mature trend |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| Distance from the 200-EMA | Small | Large (often >3 ATR) |
| Prior trend | Opposite/reversal | Same, extended |
| Volume | Expanding | Flat or declining |
| Pullbacks | Shallow, bought quickly | Deeper, slower to recover |
| Momentum | Strong and persistent | Diverging |
| News/attention | Absent | Everywhere |
| Participants | Few | Crowded |

Practical consequence: **early-trend pullbacks are the higher-quality continuation entries; mature-trend
breakouts are the lower-quality ones.** Same pattern, different quality.

## 5. Transition markers

Between markup and distribution (and markdown/accumulation): a range. Watch for:

- ADX falling from >30 to <20
- Bollinger bandwidth compressing
- Equal highs (or equal lows) forming
- Pullbacks getting deeper and recoveries slower
- Volume on the trend side declining

These are observations, not a signal. They mean "the trend is no longer extending" — which is different from "the
trend has reversed".

## 6. Applying it

```
Step 1  Identify the prior phase (what did price just do?)
Step 2  Identify the current behaviour (trending or ranging?)
Step 3  If ranging, note what preceded it and the volume trend
Step 4  Assign a lean, explicitly labelled as a lean
Step 5  Define the resolution triggers and invalidations for both directions
Step 6  Size according to uncertainty — in an ambiguous phase, size down or stand aside
```

## 7. Common errors

| Error | Guard |
|-------|-------|
| Calling a range "accumulation" in real time | Use "consistent with" |
| Assuming phases have fixed durations | They do not |
| Trading a mature trend as if it were early | Check distance from the 200-EMA |
| Ignoring volume in the phase read | Volume is half the evidence |
| Forcing a phase when the market is genuinely ambiguous | "Uncertain" is a valid answer |

## 8. Beginner summary (Hinglish)

**Char phases:**
1. **Accumulation** — girawat ke baad sideway, smart log dheere dheere khareedte hain.
2. **Markup** — uptrend, HH/HL.
3. **Distribution** — upar move ke baad sideway, dheere dheere bech rahe hain.
4. **Markdown** — downtrend, LH/LL.

**Sabse important sach:** real time mein phase **pakka nahi** pata chalta. Accumulation aur distribution ka
matlab baad mein hi clear hota hai.

**Isliye:** "yeh distribution hai" mat likho. Likhо: "yeh distribution ke saath consistent hai, kyunki advance ke
baad range bani, volume gir raha hai, upper boundary 3 baar reject hui."

**Kaam ka tareeka:** range ko describe karo, dono taraf ke triggers define karo, aur size chhota rakho jab
confusion ho.

## 9. Exercise

Take a chart with a clear range after an advance. Write the objective description (range width in ATR, duration,
volume trend, touches on each boundary, distance from the 200-EMA). Then write both resolution plans. Then, only
after noting the outcome, label which phase it was — and write one sentence on whether you could have known in
advance.
