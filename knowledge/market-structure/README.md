# Market Structure — Index

Market structure is the *frame* within which price action happens: trend phases, ranges, and the mapping of
levels. It answers "what kind of market is this?" before "what is the next candle saying?"

## The four questions structure answers

1. **Is there a trend, and in which direction?**
2. **Where are the levels that matter?**
3. **Where am I in the trend's life cycle** (early, mature, exhausted)?
4. **Which timeframes agree, and which conflict?**

Answer these before analysing patterns. A perfect hammer in a mature, exhausted trend at a level that has failed
three times is not the same object as a hammer in an early trend at fresh support.
