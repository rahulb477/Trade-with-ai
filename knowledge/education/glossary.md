# Glossary — English and Hinglish

One line each. Terms marked ⚠️ carry a common misunderstanding.

## Candles and price

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| Open | First traded price in the interval | Candle ki shuruaati price |
| High | Highest price in the interval | Sabse upar ki price |
| Low | Lowest price in the interval | Sabse neeche ki price |
| Close | Last traded price in the interval — the most important field | Candle ki aakhri price — sabse important |
| Body | Distance between open and close | Open aur close ke beech ka hissa |
| Wick / shadow | The thin line beyond the body | Body ke bahar ki patli line |
| Bullish candle | Close above open | Green candle — close open se upar |
| Bearish candle | Close below open | Red candle — close open se neeche |
| Doji | Open ≈ close | Jisme body na ke barabar ho |
| Marubozu | No wicks | Jisme wick na ho |
| Range | High − low | Ek candle ki total movement |

## Structure

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| Support | A price where buyers have previously appeared | Woh price jahan buyers aate hain |
| Resistance | A price where sellers have previously appeared | Woh price jahan sellers aate hain |
| Level | A price with ≥2 reactions | Woh price jahan 2+ baar reaction hua |
| Zone | A price band, not a single line | Ek price band, line nahi |
| Higher high (HH) | A peak above the previous peak | Pichhle high se upar ka high |
| Higher low (HL) | A trough above the previous trough | Pichhle low se upar ka low |
| Lower high (LH) | A peak below the previous peak | Pichhle high se neeche ka high |
| Lower low (LL) | A trough below the previous trough | Pichhle low se neeche ka low |
| Uptrend | HH + HL | Upar jaane wala trend |
| Downtrend | LH + LL | Neeche jaane wala trend |
| Range / sideways | No sustained HH or LL | Sideways — na upar na neeche |
| Swing high | A local peak (defined by k bars each side) | Local top |
| Swing low | A local trough | Local bottom |
| Trendline | A line through ≥2 swing points | Do ya zyada points se bani line |

## Events

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| Breakout ⚠️ | A **close** beyond resistance | Resistance ke upar **close** — wick nahi |
| Breakdown ⚠️ | A **close** below support | Support ke neeche **close** |
| Retest | Return to a broken level to check it holds | Toote hue level par wapas aana |
| Rejection | Price refused at a level, closed away | Level par price accept nahi hui |
| Fakeout | A break that reverses | Jhootha breakout |
| Bull trap ⚠️ | An upside break that fails | Aisa breakout jo baad mein fail ho jaye |
| Bear trap ⚠️ | A downside break that fails | Aisa breakdown jo baad mein fail ho jaye |
| Liquidity sweep | A wick beyond a level that closes back inside | Level ke bahar wick, close andar |
| Consolidation | Sideways compression | Sideways compression |
| Pullback | Counter-trend move inside a trend | Trend ke andar thoda ulta move |
| Reversal | Structure break changing trend direction | Trend ki direction badalna |
| Gap | Price jumps between two prints | Price ka jump |
| Choppy | Noisy, directionless | Bina direction ka noise |

## Indicators

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| MA / SMA / EMA | Average of closes; EMA weights recent more | Average price line; EMA naye price ko zyada weight deta hai |
| RSI ⚠️ | 0–100 momentum oscillator; >70 is "overbought" but not a sell signal | Momentum meter — 70+ ka matlab sell nahi |
| MACD | Difference of two EMAs plus a signal line | Do EMAs ka difference |
| ATR ⚠️ | Average true range — volatility, not direction | Volatility ka measure — direction nahi |
| ADX ⚠️ | Trend **strength**, not direction | Trend kitna strong hai — direction nahi |
| Bollinger Bands | Volatility envelope around a 20-SMA | Volatility ke around bands |
| VWAP | Volume-weighted average price, resets daily | Din ka volume-weighted average price |
| Supertrend | ATR-based trailing trend line | ATR based trend line |
| OBV | Cumulative volume by price direction | Volume ka cumulative line |
| Divergence ⚠️ | Price and indicator disagree; a hypothesis, not a signal | Price aur indicator ka disagreement — signal nahi |
| Overbought / oversold ⚠️ | Indicator extremes; not automatic reversal | Indicator extreme — reversal guarantee nahi |

## Risk and execution

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| Entry | The price you would transact at | Jahan se trade lena hai |
| Stop loss ⚠️ | The price that proves the idea wrong | Predefined risk limit — idea galat sabit hone ka price |
| Target / take profit | A planned exit level | Planned profit level |
| R | Risk unit; 1R = entry-to-stop distance | Ek risk unit |
| R:R / risk-reward | Reward per unit of risk | Risk ke badle reward ka ratio |
| Position size | Units = risk amount ÷ risk per unit | Kitne shares lene hain |
| Invalidation | The condition that voids the setup | Setup khatam hone ki condition |
| Slippage ⚠️ | Difference between expected and filled price | Expected aur actual fill price ka farq |
| Gap risk ⚠️ | Price can jump past your stop | Stop se bahut aage open ho sakta hai |
| Drawdown | Fall from an equity peak | Peak se girawat |
| Notional | Units × price | Total exposure value |
| Leverage | Exposure greater than capital | Capital se zyada exposure |
| Expectancy | Average R per trade | Per trade average R |

## Market context

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| NIFTY 50 | NSE's 50-stock benchmark | NSE ka main index |
| BANK NIFTY | NSE's banking index | Bank stocks ka index |
| Sensex | BSE's 30-stock benchmark | BSE ka main index |
| FII / FPI | Foreign institutional investors | Videshi investors |
| DII | Domestic institutional investors | Ghar ke institutional investors |
| OI ⚠️ | Open interest: contracts outstanding | Khule contracts — bullish/bearish signal nahi |
| PCR | Put/call open interest ratio | Put OI ÷ call OI |
| IV ⚠️ | Implied volatility: option expensiveness | Option kitna mehnga hai |
| Max pain ⚠️ | Strike minimising option payouts | Calculation, prediction nahi |
| Expiry | Contract expiration date | Contract khatam hone ki date — schedule verify karo |
| Circuit limit | Exchange price band | Price band |
| Delivery % | Share of volume taken for delivery | Kitna volume delivery ke liye gaya |

## Research terms

| Term | English | Hinglish |
|------|---------|----------|
| Backtest | Running rules on historical data | Purane data par rules test karna |
| Overfitting ⚠️ | Rules fitted to noise | Rules sample ko yaad kar gaye |
| Out-of-sample | Data not used during design | Woh data jo design mein use nahi hua |
| Win rate | Winners / total | Jeetne ka percentage |
| Profit factor | Gross profit / gross loss | Profit ÷ loss |
| Expectancy | Average R per trade | Per trade average R |
| Sharpe ratio | Return per unit of volatility | Return vs volatility |
| Max drawdown | Worst peak-to-trough | Sabse badi girawat |
| Confidence interval | Range that likely contains the true value | Sahi value ka possible range |

⚠️ = a term that beginners most often misuse. The Hinglish column deliberately states the *correct* meaning where
the misconception is common.
