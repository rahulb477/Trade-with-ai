# Rising Wedge

**Type:** Reversal / distribution (bearish implication) · **Bars:** typically 15–60

## 1. Shape
**Both** boundaries rise, and they **converge** — the upper line rises more slowly than the lower line, or the
lower line rises faster. Price is making higher highs and higher lows, but in a narrowing channel losing
momentum.

```
            ╱╱╱   converging
        ╱╱╱
    ╱╱╱
 ╱╱╱
```

## 2. Formation rules
| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | Both boundaries have a positive slope |
| 2 | They converge (the channel narrows over time) |
| 3 | ≥2 touches on each boundary |
| 4 | Often follows an uptrend, but can appear inside a larger decline (a bear rally) |
| 5 | Volume typically **contracts** as the wedge rises — the rise is losing participation |
| 6 | Confirmation: a **close below the lower boundary** |

## 3. Why a rising pattern is bearish
This is the pattern that confuses beginners most. Interpretation: price is still grinding up, but each push
requires more effort and achieves less. The narrowing channel is a **loss of momentum**, not strength. When the
lower boundary breaks, the buyers who kept buying the higher lows are trapped.

**Counter-case:** a rising wedge is *not* automatically bearish. In a very strong trend, a shallow rising wedge
can be a continuation pattern that resolves upward with a sharp acceleration. The confirmation (which way the
lower boundary breaks) is what decides. State the bias, take the confirmation.

## 4. Volume
Contracting volume as price rises is the core evidence. Rising volume inside a rising wedge contradicts the
distribution reading — re-examine whether the pattern is actually a channel.

## 5. Confirmation and entry
```
Confirmation : close below the lower boundary of the wedge
Entry        : the breakdown close, or a failed retest of the broken lower line
Volume       : expansion on the breakdown
Note         : if the upper boundary breaks instead, the wedge has resolved *upward* — flip the interpretation
```

## 6. Measurement
```
height = widest part of the wedge (usually at the start)
target = breakdown point − height
```
Worked: start width 30 (e.g. 620 high, 590 low), breakdown at 646 → target 616.

## 7. Invalidation
Close back above the lower boundary after the break; a decisive break above the upper boundary (an upward
resolution — the pattern becomes a continuation); the wedge's lower line never breaks and price simply keeps
grinding.

## 8. Common false signals
| False signal | Guard |
|--------------|-------|
| A rising channel (parallel lines) called a wedge | Require convergence |
| Wedge drawn with only one touch per side | Require ≥2 |
| Volume expanding on the rise | Contradicts the reading |
| Shorting inside the wedge before the break | Wait for the close below the lower line |
| Ignoring a strong higher-timeframe uptrend | Context check; wedges inside strong trends often resolve up |
| Rising wedge on a 1-minute chart | Noise |

## 9. Worked example (fictional)
```
Instrument BCD · 1D · rally 520 → 596 over 5 weeks in a narrowing channel
Upper line: 570 → 585 → 592 (slope flattening)
Lower line: 545 → 570 → 582 (steeper)
Volume: declining from 2.1M to 0.8M across the pattern ✔
Breakdown: close 578 (below the lower line at 581) on 1.6× volume → CONFIRMED
Width at the start = 570 − 545 = 25 → measured target 553
Structural target: prior swing low 560 (nearer)
Invalidation: close above 594 (upper line + buffer)
Retest entry 580 · stop 594 · risk 14 · T1 560 = 1.43R (marginal) · T2 553 = 1.93R ✔
Verdict: marginal on T1 — acceptable only with T2 as the primary target. Say so.
```

## 10. Beginner summary (Hinglish)
**Rising wedge:** dono lines **upar** ja rahi hain, par ek doosre ke kareeb aa rahi hain (converge).

**Matlab:** price upar to ja raha hai, par har naya high zyada mehnat se aur kam volume par ban raha hai —
momentum kam ho raha hai. Isliye yeh **bearish** bias rakhta hai.

**Signal:** neeche wali line ke **neeche close** hone par.

**Target:** wedge ki sabse badi width, breakdown se neeche project.

**Zaroori:** agar upar wali line toot jaye, to pattern **upar** resolve hua hai — matlab bearish reading galat
thi. Isliye confirmation ka wait karo, guess mat karo.

clear in advance or only obvious afterwards.
