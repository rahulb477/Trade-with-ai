# Trend Reversal

## 1. Definition
A **reversal** is a change in the direction of the established trend, confirmed by a break of structure. It is
distinct from a pullback: a pullback holds the structure, a reversal breaks it.

## 2. Reversal vs pullback — the only reliable test

| Aspect | Pullback | Reversal |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Prior HL/LL | Holds | Breaks (close beyond) |
| Next swing | New HH (uptrend) | Lower high forms |
| Volume | Lower on the counter-move | Often higher on the counter-move |
| Duration | Short relative to the trend | Comparable or longer |
| Confirmation | Trend resumes | Structure change + failed retest |

**The single decisive test is structural**: has a close occurred beyond the last significant swing in the
direction opposite the trend? If not, it is a pullback until proven otherwise.

## 3. Stages of a reversal (documented characterisation)

```
Stage 1  Trend runs; participants are comfortable
Stage 2  Momentum diverges; the trend makes a marginal new extreme on less volume
Stage 3  Distribution/accumulation: range forms, ADX falls
Stage 4  Structure breaks: LH after HH (or HL after LL) — this is the CHoCH in smart-money framing
Stage 5  Retest of the broken structure fails
Stage 6  New trend establishes HH/HL (or LH/LL)
```

Most beginners try to trade Stage 2 or 3. The reliable evidence appears at Stage 4–5.

## 4. Evidence classes for a reversal

| Class | Example | Weight |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Structural | Close beyond the last HL / LH | Highest |
| Pattern | H&S, double top, triple top completed | High, needs breakout |
| Volume | Distribution at the highs, climax at the lows | Medium |
| Momentum | Divergence on RSI/MACD | Low alone — very common and often premature |
| Volatility | ATR expanding, Bollinger widening | Context only |
| Sentiment/context | News, positioning (only with real data) | Medium, must be cited |

**The most common error** is treating momentum divergence as a reversal signal. Divergences can persist for
months in strong trends. They are a *warning to tighten risk*, not an entry.

## 5. Entry after a reversal is confirmed

```
Long (reversal up):
  confirmation : close above the last LH, or a completed inverse H&S neckline break
  entry        : on the confirmation close, or on a retest of the broken level that holds
  invalidation : below the reversal low (the head/LL)
  targets      : measured move of the pattern; prior swing highs; the 50/200 EMA above

Short (reversal down): mirror.
```

Reversal stops are typically **wide** because the pattern's extreme is far from the entry. This is why reversal
R:R is often worse than continuation R:R even when the direction is right. Compute it; do not assume.

## 6. Why reversals are the hardest category

1. **They are rarer than continuations** on any given bar.
2. **Confirmation is late** by design — you must wait for structure to break.
3. **Stops are wide**, so sizing shrinks.
4. **The first reversal attempt usually fails.** Markets typically test a level multiple times before turning.

A practical rule: after a clear trend, the *first* counter-trend structure break is often a trap. The second,
with a failed retest, is more reliable on average. This is a tendency, not a law.

## 7. Failure modes

| Failure | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Structure breaks, then price reclaims and makes a new HH | The reversal failed; the trend resumes (a "false reversal") |
| Divergence with no structural break | The trend continues; the divergence was noise |
| Reversal pattern completes but immediately stalls at the prior extreme | Insufficient demand/supply to start a trend |
| Multiple reversal attempts in a row | Each one traps counter-trend traders |

## 8. Beginner summary (Hinglish)

**Reversal kya hai?** Trend ki **direction badal** jana — par confirm hone ke baad.

**Pullback aur reversal mein farq:** pullback mein structure **hold** karta hai (last HL/LH intact rehta hai).
Reversal mein structure **toot** jata hai (close, wick nahi).

**Sabse bada trap:** RSI divergence dekh kar reversal maan lena. Divergence hafton tak chal sakta hai. Asli
evidence structure break hai.

**Isliye:** reversal sabse mushkil category hai — late confirmation, wide stop. Pehli koshish aksar fail hoti
hai; doosri (failed retest ke saath) zyada reliable hoti hai.

**Sabse important practical baat:** reversal R:R aksar kharab hota hai — calculation karo, feel par mat chalo.

## 9. Exercise

Find one reversal that worked and one that failed. For each, note: the structural break, whether the break was a
close, whether there was a retest, the stop distance in ATR, and the R:R. Then write the check that would have
flagged the failure in advance — or state honestly that nothing would have, and that only position sizing
protected you.
