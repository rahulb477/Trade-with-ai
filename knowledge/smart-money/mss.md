# MSS — Market Structure Shift

**Plain language:** a change of character **with displacement** — a decisive, fast counter-trend break, rather
than a slow grind. Some SMC material uses MSS and CHoCH interchangeably; others require displacement for MSS.
State which definition you are using.

## 1. Definition (as used here)

```
MSS = counter-trend close beyond the last opposing swing
      AND displacement (a wide-range bar, e.g. >=1.2 x ATR, or a gap)
      AND a subsequent failure to reclaim the broken level
```

All three conditions matter. Without displacement, it is a CHoCH. Without the failed reclaim, it is unconfirmed.

## 2. Why displacement matters

A slow drift through a swing low can be:

- a liquidity sweep (a wick that closes back),
- low-volume noise in a quiet session,
- an orderly pullback that continues.

A **displacement** move — a wide-range candle closing decisively beyond, often leaving an imbalance behind —
indicates genuine aggression. It is the closest thing to evidence of intent that a chart can show, and even that
is an interpretation.

## 3. Reading it

```
1. Identify the trend and the last opposing swing.
2. Look for a wide-range bar closing beyond that swing.
3. Note whether the bar left an imbalance (see fair-value-gaps.md).
4. Watch the next 1-3 bars: do they reclaim the broken level?
   - no  -> MSS accepted; look for the first pullback in the new direction
   - yes -> failed; the old trend likely resumes
```

## 4. Entry framing (hypothetical)

```
After an accepted MSS:
  Long (after a bearish trend shifted bullish):
    entry      : pullback into the origin zone of the displacement leg (the last down candle before the shift)
    invalidation: below the MSS low
    targets    : the prior swing highs; the origin of the prior down leg
  Quality filters: volume on the displacement bar; higher timeframe not strongly opposed; room >= 2 ATR
```

Note the entry is on a **pullback**, not on the MSS bar itself. Entering the MSS bar means chasing a wide-range
candle with a far stop.

## 5. Failure modes

| Failure | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Immediate reclaim of the broken level | The MSS was a sweep |
| No pullback at all, price runs | Missed trade — that is acceptable; do not chase |
| Displacement bar on low volume | Suspect; needs confirmation |
| MSS against a strong higher-timeframe trend | Higher failure rate |

## 6. Common misuses

| Misuse | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Calling any CHoCH an MSS | Requires displacement |
| Using MSS to skip the higher timeframe | Context still governs |
| Assuming the origin zone will hold | It is a zone you drew, not a guarantee |
| Entering on the MSS bar with a tight stop | The stop is inside the displacement bar's noise |

## 7. Report wording

> ✅ "A 1.8-ATR bearish bar closed at 1,178, below the prior higher low at 1,186, and the next two bars failed to
> reclaim it. That is a structure shift with displacement on the 15m. The origin zone of that bar (1,180–1,188)
> is the reference for a pullback entry; invalidation is below 1,172."
>
> ❌ "MSS confirmed, institutions are short."

## 8. Beginner summary (Hinglish)

**MSS = Market Structure Shift.** CHoCH jaisa hi, par **tez aur bade** move ke saath (displacement) — slow grind
nahi.

**Teen cheezein chahiye:**
1. Pichhle swing ke against **close**.
2. Woh candle **badi** ho (1.2+ ATR).
3. Agli 1–3 candles wapas us level ko reclaim **na** karein.

**Entry:** MSS candle par nahi (chasing). Pullback par, us displacement leg ke origin zone mein.

**Stop:** MSS ki low ke neeche.

**Galti:** sirf CHoCH ko MSS bol dena. Displacement chahiye.
